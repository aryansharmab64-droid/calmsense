What CalmSense offers

Six tools that work together, from raw measurements to everyday habits.

Personalized stress estimation

A trained classifier estimates Low, Moderate or High stress indicators from your measurements.

Wearable sensor monitoring

Heart rate, HRV, skin temperature and EDA, from a labeled simulation mode or structured input.

Historical trends

Filter by day, week or month and see how your signals change over time.

Sleep and activity insights

Compare sleep and movement with your stress estimates across recent days.

Guided breathing

An in-browser breathing circle with a comfortable pace and no forced breath-holding.

Personalized suggestions

A transparent, rule-based engine suggests small, optional wellness activities.

How it works

From a raw reading to a cautious, explained estimate.

  1. Collect or simulate sensor measurements.
  2. Validate and preprocess values and timestamps.
  3. Compare with your personal baseline.
  4. Generate a stress-level estimate.
  5. Explain the contributing features.
  6. Suggest appropriate wellness activities.
  7. Track trends over time.

Benefits of self-awareness

Noticing patterns in sleep, movement and recovery can help you reflect on routines. It is awareness, not a verdict.

Privacy-first approach

Data stays in a local database on the machine running the app. Records are private to each account and can be deleted.

Responsible use

Exercise, caffeine, illness, medication and individual differences all affect wearable readings. Estimates can be wrong.
